## 1. Overview

Ankesh Software Solutions is a modern software company providing innovative solutions for Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Contract Management.

The Customer & Contract Management System (CCMS) helps organizations efficiently manage customer information, contracts, agreements, renewals, approvals, and related documents through a centralized platform.

The system simplifies business operations by allowing users to:

- Create and maintain customer records
- Generate and track contracts
- Upload supporting documents
- Monitor contract status
- Receive notifications for upcoming renewals

With user-friendly dashboards and reporting features, organizations gain better visibility into their customer base and contract lifecycle.

## 3. How-To Guides

### 3.1 Creating a Customer

1. Navigate to the **Customer Management** module.
2. Select **Add Customer**.
3. Enter the required information:
   - Customer name
   - Contact person
   - Phone number
   - Email address
   - Billing address
   - Tax details (if applicable)
4. Review the information and click **Save**.
5. The system automatically generates a unique **Customer ID**, which can be used for future transactions and contract creation.

### 3.2 Creating a Contract

**Prerequisite:** The customer record must already exist in the system.

1. Navigate to the **Contract Management** module.
2. Select **Create Contract**.
3. Choose the customer.
4. Enter contract details:
   - Contract number
   - Start date
   - End date
   - Service type
   - Contract value
   - Billing frequency
5. Upload supporting documents (agreements, purchase orders, work orders).
6. Verify all details.
7. Submit the contract for approval or activation, as per your organization's workflow.

### 3.3 Searching for a Customer

Go to **Customer Management** and search using any of:

- Customer Name
- Customer ID
- Email Address
- Mobile Number

### 3.4 Editing Customer Information

Open the customer record → click **Edit** → update the required fields → save the changes.

### 3.5 Renewing a Contract

Open the contract → click **Renew Contract** → enter the new contract details → save the renewal.

### 3.6 Viewing All Contracts for a Customer

Open the customer profile and navigate to the **Contracts** section to view all linked contracts.

### 3.7 Downloading Documents

Open the contract's document section and click **Download** for the required document.

### 3.8 Raising a Support Ticket

Navigate to **Help Desk → Raise Ticket**, or contact the Ankesh Software Solutions support team.

### 3.9 Resetting a Forgotten Password

Click **Forgot Password** on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

## 4. Contract Lifecycle

Every contract passes through the following stages:

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Draft** | Contract is being prepared. |
| **Submitted** | Sent for approval. |
| **Approved** | Approved by authorized users. |
| **Active** | Contract is currently valid. |
| **Expired** | Contract validity has ended. |
| **Renewed** | Contract has been extended. |
| **Closed** | Contract has been completed or terminated. |

Users can track the current status of each contract from the **Contract Management dashboard**.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Customers

**Q1. How do I create a new customer?**
Navigate to Customer Management → Add Customer, enter the required customer details, and click Save. A unique Customer ID will be generated automatically.

**Q2. How do I search for a customer?**
Go to Customer Management and search using the Customer Name, Customer ID, Email Address, or Mobile Number.

**Q3. Can I edit customer information after creation?**
Yes. Open the customer record, click Edit, update the required fields, and save the changes.

**Q4. Can one customer have multiple contracts?**
Yes. A customer can have multiple active or inactive contracts.

**Q5. Can I view all contracts for a customer?**
Yes. Open the customer profile and navigate to the Contracts section to view all linked contracts.

### Contracts

**Q6. Can I create a contract without creating a customer first?**
No. A customer record must exist before a contract can be created.

**Q7. How do I create a new contract?**
Go to Contract Management → Create Contract, select the customer, enter contract details, upload documents if required, and click Submit.

**Q8. What do the contract statuses mean?**
- Draft: Contract is being prepared.
- Submitted: Sent for approval.
- Approved: Approved by authorized users.
- Active: Contract is currently valid.
- Expired: Contract validity has ended.
- Renewed: Contract has been extended.
- Closed: Contract has been completed or terminated.

**Q9. How can I renew a contract?**
Open the contract, click Renew Contract, enter the new contract details, and save the renewal.

**Q10. How do I know if a contract is about to expire?**
The dashboard and expiry reports display contracts nearing their expiration date.

**Q11. What happens when a contract expires?**
The contract status changes to Expired and can be renewed or closed based on business requirements.

**Q12. Who can approve contracts?**
Only users with approval permissions or designated approvers can approve contracts.

**Q13. Can I track all activities performed on a contract?**
Yes. The system maintains an activity log showing creation, updates, approvals, and document uploads.

**Q14. What should I do if I entered incorrect information?**
Open the customer or contract record and update the information if your role has edit access. Otherwise, contact the system administrator.

### Documents

**Q15. What documents can be uploaded with a contract?**
Contract Agreement, Purchase Order (PO), Work Order (WO), Amendment Documents, Renewal Documents, and Supporting Files.

**Q16. Can I upload documents after contract creation?**
Yes. Additional documents can be uploaded anytime, subject to user permissions.

**Q17. Can I download uploaded documents?**
Yes. Open the contract's document section and click Download for the required document.

### Reports & Support

**Q18. Can I generate reports from the system?**
Yes. Available reports include Customer Reports, Active Contract Reports, Expiry Reports, Renewal Reports, and Contract Value Reports.

**Q19. How do I contact support?**
Navigate to Help Desk → Raise Ticket or contact the Ankesh Software Solutions support team.

**Q20. I forgot my password. What should I do?**
Click Forgot Password on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.
